Projects

Explore how we apply technology to solve real-world problems in Malawi and beyond.

AI for Film Classification

We partnered with the Malawi Censorship Board to digitize legacy paper-based film scripts and implement an AI-enabled classification system to support age-rating decisions. The platform streamlines the entire submission and review workflow, accelerating turnaround times, enhancing operational efficiency, and reducing paper consumption in line with environmentally sustainable practices.

Record Linkage Tool

We built an AI-powered tool to automatically link African names across different datasets, enabling better identity resolution and data integration in health and research systems.
